DialMaster — Pre-Registration Reward Terms

One-time reward (500 bonus coins) for users who pre-register and install the app.

Eligibility

To be eligible for the pre-registration reward:

Pre-registration
User must pre-register through Google Play.
Account
Reward is granted only once per Google account.
Delivery
Reward is automatically delivered via Google Play upon first eligible login after installation.

Reward

The pre-registration reward details are:

  • Amount: 500 bonus coins (one-time).
  • Trigger: First installation and first successful login after official launch.
  • Automatic delivery: The reward is delivered automatically by Google Play.

Conditions

Non-transferable
The 500 coins are non-transferable.
No cash value
The reward has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.
Google Play
Google Play terms apply.
Modification
DialMaster reserves the right to modify or discontinue the promotion if required.

Reward delivery

Delivery process:

  • Reward will be granted automatically upon first successful login after installation.
  • If you do not receive the reward, check that you used the same Google account for pre-registration and installation.

Support: For assistance, contact support@dialmaster.in.
